QuantEcon Recommended Chapters

Asset & Liability Management — Savings and Retirement Investment Focus

Core Must-Read: Directly Models ALM Problems
Chapter Series Why It Matters for ALM
Ch 12 — Consumption Smoothing FIRST Core ALM intuition: matching spending liabilities to asset income over time.
Ch 75 — The Permanent Income Model INTER Formal model of optimal saving/consumption under income uncertainty — the backbone of retirement planning theory.
Ch 76 — Permanent Income II: LQ Techniques INTER Solves the permanent income model computationally; shows how to implement lifecycle savings strategies.
Ch 81 — Consumption Smoothing with Incomplete and Complete Markets INTER Models how access to financial instruments (bonds, annuities) changes optimal retirement portfolios.
Ch 67–68 — Income Fluctuation Problem I & II INTER Household buffer-stock saving under income risk — directly relevant to defined-contribution retirement accounts.
Ch 61–65 — Optimal Savings I, II, III, IV, V (Cake Eating) INTER Step-by-step build-up of the finite-horizon drawdown problem; models decumulation (spending down a retirement pot).
Ch 94 — The Aiyagari Model INTER Heterogeneous-agent model of wealth accumulation under uninsurable income risk — standard framework for pension fund design analysis.
High Value Asset Pricing & Portfolio Theory
Chapter Series Why It Matters for ALM
Ch 42 — Elementary Asset Pricing Theory ADV State-price deflators, no-arbitrage pricing — essential for valuing pension liabilities and annuities.
Ch 45 — Two Modifications of Mean-Variance Portfolio Theory ADV Directly addresses portfolio construction; mean-variance is the standard ALM optimisation framework.
Ch 99 — Asset Pricing: Finite State Models INTER Pricing bonds and equity in discrete settings; needed for liability-matching bond portfolios.
Ch 100 — Stochastic Consumption, Risk Aversion & Asset Returns INTER Links investor risk aversion to required returns — core to setting long-run return assumptions in retirement plans.
Ch 44 — The Hansen-Jagannathan Bound ADV Benchmark for evaluating whether a portfolio's risk/return is consistent with any rational preference model.
Ch 102 — Competitive Equilibria with Arrow Securities INTER Complete markets benchmark for hedging longevity and income risks in pension design.
Context Risk, Uncertainty & Robustness
Chapter Series Why It Matters for ALM
Ch 28 — Risk and Model Uncertainty ADV Distinguishes risk (known distribution) from ambiguity — critical for long-horizon retirement planning where distributions are unknown.
Ch 30 — Robustness ADV How to make savings/investment decisions that are robust to model misspecification — directly applicable to stress-testing ALM models.
Ch 82 — Robust Consumption Smoothing and Precautionary Savings INTER Precautionary saving motive under ambiguity — explains why retirement buffers exist beyond pure optimization.
Ch 34 — AR(1) Processes FIRST Foundation for modelling interest rates, asset returns, and wage processes used in all lifecycle models.
Ch 38 — Finite Markov Chains INTER Models regime-switching in asset returns and employment states — standard in stochastic ALM scenario generation.
Ch 98 — Two Computations to Fund Social Security INTER Directly models public pension funding — most applicable chapter to institutional retirement fund management.
Suggested reading order: Start with First Course Ch 12 (intuition) → Intermediate Ch 61–65 (cake-eating / decumulation) → Ch 75–76 (permanent income) → Ch 67–68 (income fluctuation) → Ch 81, 94 (markets & heterogeneity) → then branch into Asset Pricing (Ch 99–100, Adv Ch 42, 45) and Robustness (Adv Ch 28, 30) as needed.
